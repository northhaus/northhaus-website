What do I send you? +

For an audit, the address of one public page. For brand files, a link to your logo; a clear PNG or SVG works best. You add both at checkout on Gumroad.

Can I use the files for my business? +

Yes. You can use every file in your kit for your business, for as long as you like.

What happens to my details? +

We keep your audit details for 30 days after delivery, and audits are prepared using AI tools. We delete your logo file 30 days after delivery.

What if something goes wrong? +

If a report arrives late or misreads your page, or a file is faulty, email us and we’ll fix it or refund you. Your statutory rights aren’t affected.
