What we collect and why

1. When you use the contact form

What: your name, email address and message, plus anything else you choose to include.
Why: to read and reply to your enquiry.
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or, if you're asking about working with us, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

2. When you email us directly

What: your email address, name and whatever you write.
Why and legal basis: as above.

3. If you become a client

What: your contact details, project correspondence, invoices, and the payment details that appear on our bank statement when you pay by bank transfer (for example the account name and reference).
Why: to deliver the work, invoice you and keep business records.
Legal basis: performing our contract with you, and complying with legal obligations such as tax record-keeping.

4. If you buy a digital product

Purchases are made through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects and processes your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy (https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy). We do not see your full card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your name, email address and order details with us so we can support your purchase.
Legal basis: performing the contract for the product you bought, and our legitimate interest in providing customer support.

5. When you visit the website

Our hosting provider automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

We don't use your data for marketing, we don't sell it, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: 12 months from our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client records, including invoices and correspondence: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, to meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Digital product order records: kept by Lemon Squeezy under its own policy. Any copies we hold are kept for 6 years for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Hosting logs: kept by Vercel for a short period under its own retention policy.

Cookies

This website does not currently set any cookies or use similar tracking technologies, and we do not use analytics. Our font and script providers (Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs) receive your IP address when they deliver files to your browser, but they do not set cookies on this site.

If this changes we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable format, where applicable

To make a request, email northhaus.io@outlook.com. We'll respond within one month. There's normally no charge.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
