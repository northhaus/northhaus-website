Four steps to a live site.

KEY TERMS

The terms, in plain English.

  1. Paid monthly by Direct Debit. Nothing is paid upfront. You set up a Direct Debit once you’ve approved the design, and we collect your first payment when you approve the finished site, before it goes live. Your payments are covered by the Direct Debit Guarantee.

  2. Minimum term. 12 months. After that it rolls monthly, and you can leave with 30 days’ notice.

  3. Leaving early. You pay the months left in your first 12.

  4. Your domain. It’s registered in your name and we pay for it while you’re on the plan. If you leave, it goes with you. Any domain over £20 a year is charged at cost.

  5. Ownership. Your content, logo, photos and domain are always yours. We own the design and code until you’ve completed 12 months and paid the buyout.

  6. If you leave without buying. We take the site down 14 days after your notice ends and send your content back within 14 days.

  7. Design feedback. Three rounds of feedback on the design are included before your site goes live.

  8. Replies. We reply to your messages within one working day.

  9. Changes. Up to 30 minutes a month. That covers edits to what’s already there. Unused time doesn’t roll over.

  10. Business clients only. The plan is for UK sole traders and limited companies buying for their business, one site per business. You confirm this when you sign up.

GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know.

Do I pay anything before the site goes live? +

No. Once you’ve approved the design, you set up a monthly Direct Debit. We collect the first payment when you approve the finished site, just before it goes live.

What counts as a change? +

Edits to what’s already on your site: text, prices, photos, links, opening hours and gallery items. You get up to 30 minutes a month. We quote new pages, sections or features before we start.

Can I cancel? +

Yes, after your first 12 months, with 30 days’ notice. If you leave before then, you pay the months left in your first 12.

I already have a domain. Can I use it? +

Yes. We connect it to your new site and you keep paying your current provider for it.

What happens to my site if I leave? +

You can buy it for £1,990, less £20 for every month you’ve paid (minimum £395). If you don’t, we take it down 14 days after your notice ends and send your content back.

What if I need more than five pages, or a shop? +

Then a paid build suits you better. Websites start from £1,990, and we give you a fixed quote before we start.

Can I sign up for a personal site? +

No. The Site Plan is for sole traders and limited companies, for their business.
