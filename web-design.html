FAQ

Web design FAQs.

How much does a website cost? +

Every Northhaus site is custom-built, so the price depends on scope. Most small-business sites start in the low four figures. Send over your requirements and I will give you a fixed quote.

How long does it take to build a website? +

A typical brochure site takes around three to six weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how ready your content is and the rounds of feedback.

Are your websites mobile-friendly and responsive? +

Yes. Every site is hand-coded to work perfectly across mobile, tablet and desktop, built on solid accessibility and performance fundamentals.

Will my website be optimised for SEO? +

Yes. Sites ship with clean semantic HTML, fast load times, structured data and per-page metadata, so they are ready to rank and to be understood by AI search engines.

Do you provide support after launch? +

Yes. I stay on hand after launch for fixes and tweaks, and can arrange ongoing maintenance if you need it.
