FAQ

Product FAQs.

What are Northhaus HTML planners? +

They are self-contained HTML tools you download and open in any web browser. There is no app to install, no account to create and no subscription. Everything stays on your own computer.

Are my details private? +

Yes. The planners run entirely in your browser and work offline. Your information never leaves your device and is not sent anywhere.

How do I receive my planner after buying? +

After checkout you get an instant download link. The file is yours to keep and to re-download whenever you need it.

Do the planners work on Mac and Windows? +

Yes. They open in any modern browser such as Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, on Mac, Windows and most tablets.

How much do they cost? +

The planners are £4.99 and the guides are £1.99. It is a one-off payment with no subscription.

Can I get a refund? +

Because these are instant digital downloads, refunds are not offered once a file has been downloaded. If something is not working, email me and I will help.
